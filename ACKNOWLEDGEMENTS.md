# Acknowledgements

hotpath.mbt is inspired by the product and instrumentation ideas explored in [pawurb/hotpath-rs](https://github.com/pawurb/hotpath-rs).

hotpath-rs demonstrates a useful application-profiler shape that combines explicit instrumentation, aggregation, reporting, and integrations around real application hot paths. hotpath.mbt is an independent MoonBit-first implementation with a different portability and toolchain design; it is not a mechanical source port.

Thanks to the hotpath-rs authors and contributors for making their work public.
